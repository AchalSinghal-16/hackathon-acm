ACM CodeNite 2027 Participation Guidelines & Code of Conduct:

  • Team Composition: Teams must have a minimum of 4 members and a maximum of 6 members. Cross-college teams are permitted and members can be from different institutes.
  • Student Eligibility: All members must be actively enrolled undergraduate or postgraduate students with valid institutional ID cards.
  • Originality of Work: All code and assets must be developed during the official 12-hour hackathon period on January 17, 2027. Pre-built projects, plagiarism, or copyright violations will lead to immediate disqualification.
  • Initial Registration Fee: An initial team registration fee of ₹50 is charged to register your team. Shortlisted teams selected for the final hackathon will be notified later regarding the ₹600 main event fee.
  • Code of Conduct: Participants must maintain professional decorum, respect mentors, organizers, and fellow hackers. Harassment of any form will not be tolerated.
  • Decisions: The decisions of the ACM CodeNite judging committee and organizers will be final and binding.
